Why solar dehydration?

A gentle drying approach that helps keep ingredients closer to their natural taste — with a story your customers love.

Clean, vibrant flavour

Concentrated taste and aroma for blends, beverages, bakery mixes, and seasoning bases.

Simple ingredient story

Less waste, longer life

From farm to powder

A clear, client-ready flow — with small animations as you scroll.

  1. Selection

    Choose ripe produce, then wash and prep to lock in natural colour and flavour.

  2. Solar dehydration

    Controlled airflow + solar-assisted drying to remove moisture gently.

  3. Milling & sieving

    Grind to a smooth powder and sieve for consistent texture.

  4. Packing

    Pack for freshness with options for retail, foodservice, or bulk supply.
